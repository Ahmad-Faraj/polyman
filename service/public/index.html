What each sample must give

CI runs every sample three times per release: against the new image, against the candidate before it gets traffic, and against production after the switch. Any difference fails the release.

Good to know

Compiled fresh
You don't send testlib.h, binaries or generated tests. They are made on the spot, and polyman's compile cache is ignored.
Held to the tag
Tags work as on the problem platform: MA, OK, WA, TL, ML, RE, PE, TO, RJ. What each one must do
